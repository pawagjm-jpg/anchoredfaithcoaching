The process

Four steps, in this order

Every engagement follows the same sequence — you always know where you are.

§ I

Listen

A free 20-minute call to hear what's actually going on — no framework applied yet.

§ II

Name it

We identify the specific doubt, decision, or gap you're working through, together.

§ III

Work it

Scripture, apologetics, and practical coaching applied to your actual situation — not generic advice.

§ IV

Carry it

You leave with a clearer, more durable faith — and a plan for what's next on your own.

About your coach

Justin Wagner

I've spent 18 years mentoring and coaching people — but the last two years changed how I do it. I went all in on my faith, dove head-first into the Word, and rebuilt my entire coaching model around it. I bring nearly two decades of real coaching experience together with a faith that's still fresh, still hungry, and still honest about the questions.

My faith is non-denominational and Bible-centered — Scripture as God's Word, Jesus at the center of everything, and a simple mission: helping people find and follow him. I believe faith isn't about having it all figured out; it's about showing up honest, imperfect, and willing to grow. I'm not a licensed therapist, and I'll always be upfront about where that line is.

Questions

Before you book

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. Coaching is forward-focused and faith-centered; it doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you're working through something clinical, I'll help you find a licensed therapist alongside — or instead of — coaching.

What if my doubts are pretty serious?

That's exactly who this is for. Serious, honest doubt gets a serious, honest response — not platitudes.

Do I need to belong to a specific denomination?

No. I work from a historic, mere-Christian foundation and respect where you're starting from.

What if I'm in crisis right now?

Please contact 911 or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline immediately. Coaching is not equipped to handle emergencies.
